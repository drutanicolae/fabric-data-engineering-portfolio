## 5. Design decisions

- Keep Backfill Initial separate from ongoing incremental processing.
- Keep Late Arrivals and Updated Records as independent engines with independent watermarks.
- Use `:created_at` to detect newly created source records and `:updated_at` to discover modified records.
- Use `created_date` to locate the correct monthly Bronze partition.
- Use `unique_key` as the record identity.
- Compare all 44 business fields null-safely; a changed system timestamp alone is not enough to classify a business update.
- Preserve original Bronze batches. Store late arrivals and update snapshots separately.
- Apply observed updated record states to Silver later through a MERGE keyed by `unique_key`.
